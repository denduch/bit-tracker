Privacy Policy for Bit Tracker

Bit Tracker does not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data. 

The extension:
- Stores only your tracked artists list locally on your device
- Fetches publicly available concert data from Bandsintown
- Does not track user behavior or activity
- Does not share data with third parties
- Does not use analytics or advertising

All data is stored locally using Chrome's storage API and is never sent to external servers except to Bandsintown for fetching concert information.
